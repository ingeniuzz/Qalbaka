📖 How to Play Tekka

Overview

Tekka (تیکه) is Afghanistan's most popular card game. 4 players in 2 partnerships (N/S vs W/E) using a standard 52-card deck. Play goes counter-clockwise.

Bidding

  • Starting from the dealer's right (CCW), each player bids once.
  • Legal bids: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 or Double 13 (play alone). Each bid must be higher than the last.
  • You may also Pass.
  • If everyone passes, the last bidder must bid 8.

Rang Selection

The highest bidder (Declarant) wins and must lead the first card. That card's suit becomes Rang for the entire hand.

Playing Hands

  • Follow suit if you can. If void, play any card.
  • Highest Rang card wins the hand, otherwise highest card of the led suit wins.
  • Hand winner leads the next hand.

Scoring (Target: 104 pts)

  • Bid made: Declarant's team scores the bid amount (e.g., bid 9 → +9 pts).
  • Bid failed: Opponents score 2× the bid (e.g., bid 9 fails → +18 pts to opponents).
  • Double 13 made: +26 pts. Failed: Opponents get +52 pts!
⚙ Settings
In Classic Cut, tricks won by each team are stacked, cut twice, and dealt in 13-card packets just like in authentic Tekka.
