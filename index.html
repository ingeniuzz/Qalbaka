How to Play Hearts

Goal: Have the lowest score when any player reaches 100 points!


Penalty Points:

  • Each Heart (â™¥) = 1 penalty point.
  • The Queen of Spades (â™ Q) = 13 penalty points!
  • All other cards = 0 points. (Total points in deck = 26).

Passing Cards:

At the start of each round, you choose 3 cards to pass:

  • Classic Mode: Round 1 (Left), Round 2 (Right), Round 3 (Across), Round 4 (Hold).
  • Triple Pass Mode (Custom): Every round features 3 sequential passes: Left → Front/Across → Right! You may pass cards you just received.

Trick Rules:

  • The player holding the 2 of Clubs (2â™£) must lead the first trick.
  • Players must follow suit if possible. If void, you may discard any card (except on Trick 1, no points can be played).
  • Breaking Hearts: Hearts cannot be led until someone has discarded a heart on an earlier trick.
  • The highest card of the suit led wins the trick and leads next.

Shooting the Moon:

If you take all 13 Hearts AND the Queen of Spades (all 26 points) in a single hand, you get 0 points and all three opponents get +26 penalty points!
